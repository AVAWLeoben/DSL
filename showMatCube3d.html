Rotation is sampled every 10°. Vertical movement switches between −20°, 0° and +20° elevation.

Why pre-render?

The original volshow volume can be demanding on classroom hardware. This page transfers the expensive 3D rotation/projection step to an instructor-side preprocessing step. Students receive only three compressed image atlases and need no MATLAB installation, MAT-file parser or WebGL volume renderer.
