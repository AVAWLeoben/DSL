How to read the cuboid

The large front/back faces show spatial false-colour composites. The narrow side and top/bottom faces show spatial-versus-spectral cross-sections, so the third axis represents the 42 spectral bands.

Band order, blue → red (false colour)

The spectral depth is visually exaggerated so the 42-band axis remains easy to inspect on a classroom screen.
