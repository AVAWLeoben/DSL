Greedy chooses the nearest remaining bin. Unique chooses the nearest remaining bin not already assigned to another truck. Optimize3D assigns idle trucks and bins together using three criteria: short travel distance, no duplicate targets, and balanced cumulative truck workload. City size changes the physical coordinate area; with the same bin count, a larger city means greater average distance between bins.
