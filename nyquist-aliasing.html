Core terminology

Sampling rateHow many discrete measurements are taken per second. CD audio uses 44.1 kHz.
Nyquist frequencyHalf the sampling rate. It is the upper frequency boundary of the representable baseband: 22.05 kHz for 44.1 kHz sampling.
Nyquist rateThe minimum ideal sampling rate required for a target maximum frequency: 2 × fmax. A 20 kHz tone therefore requires at least 40 kHz in the ideal theorem.
AliasingFrequencies above the Nyquist frequency fold into the baseband and appear as incorrect lower frequencies.
fN = fs/2    ·    fs,min = 2fmax

Practical note

The ideal sampling theorem assumes a band-limited signal. Real acquisition systems use an anti-alias low-pass filter before the ADC so energy above the Nyquist frequency is strongly attenuated before sampling. Practical systems also leave margin around the theoretical boundary.
