Sampling resolution, not sampling rate: this exercise varies how closely neighboring spectral measurements are spaced along the spectral axis. A smaller Δband means finer spectral sampling resolution.
The upper plot is a real 220-band spectrum from PP.mat at pixel row 97, column 33. The lower plot keeps only the selected number of evenly spaced spectral measurements and connects them linearly. With coarse sampling, local minima and narrow spectral features may be shifted, flattened, or missed entirely. This demonstrates spectral sampling resolution; it is distinct from ADC bit depth and from the instrument's optical spectral resolution/FWHM.
